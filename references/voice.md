# Your voice

Record only traits that show up in your own writing, and nothing guessed. Start from 3 to 5 pieces you wrote without AI help: emails, posts, a past write-up. Read them the way Humanizer's Voice section describes, and note each trait with the sample it came from.

## Defaults until you fill this in

- **First person.** Use "I" for your own view or work. Use "we" only for a team plan you are part of.
- **Contractions:** I'd, doesn't, it's. They read as a person talking.
- **Short hedges beside the uncertain point.** For example, "I'd set the real cut-off from the reply data". Never a blanket disclaimer.
- **Plain words over jargon.** Use, show and lead, not leverage, showcase and spearhead.
- **Spelling.** Match the spelling of the audience or the job post, and keep it consistent within one document.
- **Short paragraphs, conclusion first.**
